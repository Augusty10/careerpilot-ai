# 🚀 CareerPilot AI

### One Profile. Multiple Careers. Smarter Applications.

CareerPilot AI is an AI-powered career assistance platform designed specifically for freshers and recent graduates.

It simplifies the job-search process by allowing candidates to create multiple role-specific resumes, discover suitable job opportunities, verify companies and recruiters, and prepare customized job applications from one platform.

## 🎯 Problem

Freshers spend considerable time:
- Searching for suitable job opportunities across multiple platforms.
- Creating different resumes for different job roles.
- Understanding job eligibility requirements.
- Identifying genuine companies and recruiters.
- Repeatedly filling out job application forms.
- Tracking multiple applications.

CareerPilot AI aims to simplify this entire process.

## 💡 Our Solution

Create one master career profile and let AI assist with the complete job-search journey.

### ✨ Key Features

- **Master Career Profile:** Maintain education, skills, projects, internships and certifications in one place.
- **Multiple AI Resumes:** Generate different resumes for different job roles.
- **Smart Job Discovery:** Discover relevant fresher and entry-level opportunities.
- **AI Job Matching:** Compare candidate skills and qualifications with job requirements.
- **Company Verification:** Check company identity and available supporting evidence.
- **Recruiter Verification:** Investigate recruiter affiliation and suspicious recruitment practices.
- **Resume Customization:** Tailor resumes to individual job descriptions without inventing candidate experience.
- **Application Assistant:** Prepare application details and allow candidates to review and approve submissions.
- **Application Tracker:** Manage job applications in one dashboard.
- **Privacy Protection:** Keep resumes private and require candidate approval before sharing personal information.

## 🤖 AI Agent Architecture

CareerPilot AI uses specialized AI agents coordinated through Switch.

1. Resume Agent – Creates and customizes resumes.
2. Job Discovery Agent – Finds suitable opportunities.
3. Matching and Verification Agent – Evaluates job eligibility and recruitment credibility.
4. Application Coordinator Agent – Prepares and tracks applications.
